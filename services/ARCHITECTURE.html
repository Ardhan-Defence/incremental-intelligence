Who this is actually for

Security, fraud, and trust & safety teams vetting a counterparty, an unfamiliar IP, a suspicious domain, or an unverified social account — the kind of lookup an analyst currently does by hand across four or five separate tools. The deliverable is a credible, sourced profile an analyst acts on, not a headline a general audience scrolls past.

Not the target: a consumer-facing product, a predictive system, or a passive monitoring tool with push notifications (watchlist alerts exist, but delivery is poll-only — nothing pages anyone yet). Correlation across separate profiles doesn't exist until Entity Resolution is built; until then, two profiles that share a phone number look like two profiles, not one resolved identity.
